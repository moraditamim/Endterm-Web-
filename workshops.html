What to expect

A question is
a good starting point.

You can be new to coding, new to a topic, or just looking for a little structure.

  1. 01
    Pick a starting point.

    Choose a topic you want to practise and check the suggested starting level.

  2. 02
    Bring one question.

    It can be a concept from class, a layout that breaks, or a step you cannot explain.

  3. 03
    Make one small thing.

    Try the exercise yourself and leave with a clear next step.
